## Read this to the participant before starting

> "Work through the modules as if you were a COS212 student meeting graphs for the first time.
> Please say out loud what you're thinking as you go: what you're looking at, what you expect to
> happen, and anything that surprises or confuses you. There are no wrong answers; we're testing
> the app, not you. I won't help unless you've been stuck for a while. If you go quiet, I'll ask
> what you're thinking."

## While the participant works

- Write what they **say and do**, not what you think of it. Quote them where you can.
- If they go quiet for about 20 seconds, ask: *"What are you thinking?"*
- Only help after **two minutes stuck**, and write down that you did and what you said.
- Don't explain the design or defend it. Note the question instead.
