## 2. Ease of use: System Usability Scale (SUS)

The ten standard SUS statements (ID document [30]), with "system" read as "app". Each on a five-point
scale: 1 Strongly disagree, 2, 3, 4, 5 Strongly agree.

1. I think that I would like to use this app frequently.
2. I found the app unnecessarily complex.
3. I thought the app was easy to use.
4. I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use this app.
5. I found the various functions in this app were well integrated.
6. I thought there was too much inconsistency in this app.
7. I would imagine that most people would learn to use this app very quickly.
8. I found the app very cumbersome to use.
9. I felt very confident using the app.
10. I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this app.

**Scoring.** For odd items, the score is the answer minus 1. For even items, it is 5 minus the
answer. Add the ten scores and multiply by 2.5, giving 0 to 100 per participant. Report the mean
for the group. With seven participants who know the design, treat it as a rough signal, not a
benchmark (ID document §8.6).

## 3. Feedback and hints

Each on a five-point scale: 1 Strongly disagree, 2, 3, 4, 5 Strongly agree.

1. When an answer was wrong, the feedback told me why it was wrong.
2. The feedback told me what to check or try next.
3. The hints helped me without giving the answer away too early.
4. The warning before hint 3 made it clear what using it would mean.

- Anything about the feedback or hints that confused you? (optional, open)

## 4. Motivation: ARCS

Eight statements adapted from Keller's Instructional Materials Motivation Survey (IMMS; ID
document [38]), two for each part of the ARCS model. They are a short adaptation, not the validated
36-item IMMS, so report them as ratings per part, not as an IMMS score. Each on a five-point scale:
1 Not true, 2, 3, 4, 5 Very true.

| Part | Statement |
|---|---|
| Attention | 1. The app held my attention. |
| Attention | 2. The way the content was presented (videos, the visualiser, the activities) kept me interested. |
| Relevance | 3. It is clear to me how this content relates to what I will need in COS212. |
| Relevance | 4. The campus examples helped me see why graphs and BFS are useful. |
| Confidence | 5. As I worked through the modules, I was confident I could learn this content. |
| Confidence | 6. The hints and feedback made me feel I could get the activities right. |
| Satisfaction | 7. Completing the activities gave me a feeling of achievement. |
| Satisfaction | 8. I enjoyed working through this app. |

**Scoring.** Report the mean of each part's two statements, per participant and for the group.
